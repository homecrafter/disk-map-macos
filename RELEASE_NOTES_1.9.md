# Disk Map 1.9

The first public release of the free Disk Map utility for macOS.

Disk Map helps you understand what is taking up space on your Mac, find large files, preview them, and safely clean up storage. It runs locally without registration, ads, analytics, or transmission of file information.

Highlights:

- interactive radial disk map;
- standard and deep scanning with live progress;
- Quick Look previews and Finder integration;
- external drive support and protected formatting workflow;
- 13 interface languages;
- universal Apple Silicon and Intel build.

**Requirements:** macOS 13 or later.

**Important:** the app is not yet notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm.
